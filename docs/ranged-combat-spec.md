# Basic ranged combat — brief spec

- A short bow is equippable independently of melee weapons and automatically equips when that slot is empty.
- `R` plus an arrow fires up to six cells, hitting the first living NPC for the bow's damage.
- Firing keeps the bow equipped, consumes a turn, leaves the player stationary, and creates impact noise.
- Firing without an equipped ranged weapon or into an invalid trajectory consumes no turn.
