# NPC casualty and hazard awareness — brief spec

- NPCs remember corpses they can see and traps they are skilled enough to spot at short range.
- A newly seen corpse becomes casualty investigation evidence unless the NPC has stronger information.
- A newly seen trap is logged and treated as a known hazardous cell during pathfinding and wandering.
- Knowledge belongs to each NPC; unseen traps do not influence it, and stale trap knowledge is cleared when the NPC can verify the trap is gone.
- F1 inspection shows known hazard and casualty counts.
