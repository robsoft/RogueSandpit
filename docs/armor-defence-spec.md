# Armor and defence — brief spec

- Armor is a generated, collectible, droppable item type.
- Equipping selected armor replaces the currently equipped armor.
- Equipped armor power is the player's defence value and reduces every incoming hit.
- A successful hit always deals at least one damage.
- Combat events report actual damage after defence.
- The HUD shows equipped armor and defence; dropped equipped armor is automatically unequipped.
