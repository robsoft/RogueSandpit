# Terrain-sensitive and false trails — brief spec

- Trail clues carry strength and authenticity in addition to age and direction.
- Open-room movement creates weak clues, corridors create stronger and longer-lived clues, and doorway passage creates the strongest clues.
- `T` followed by an arrow places a false clue pointing to the adjacent cell and consumes a turn.
- False clues are weaker and shorter-lived than genuine tracks.
- Trolls and Wretches reject false clues; Goblins and Orcs may follow them; Skeletons still ignore all physical trails.
- F1 trail rendering distinguishes false clues and inspection exposes clue details.
