# NPC ranged combat — brief spec

- Goblins can fire at a visible player from two to six cardinal-distance cells away.
- A shot deals 6 base damage, respects player armor, consumes the Goblin's action, and creates combat noise at the impact.
- Terrain and closed doors continue to block line of sight; other archetypes remain melee-only.
- Events and F1 inspection identify ranged capability and shots.
